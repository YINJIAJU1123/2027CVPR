# Q1 four-system comparison (2026-11-16)

Audited Q1 release from the `supply` branch. It contains six simulated objects, two target-to-skill mappings, 40 scheduled trials per object/mapping cell, and four systems: Rule, fine-tuned Qwen, fine-tuned Qwen plus local controllers, and REVEAL. Duck and hedgehog original-mapping cells retain their registered service exclusions, leaving 38 and 39 common trials.

The main paper reports the audited success rates in `tables/q1_main_four_way.tex`; the full per-trial comparison and verification files are kept here.
