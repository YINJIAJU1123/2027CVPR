Evidence and interpretation
The Pig protocol, sample size and within-study primary analysis were frozen before formal data
collection. The follow-up was designed after the Cube result; a cross-study multiplicity family
was not prespecified. Per-object p values are unadjusted and the studies are not pooled.
Binding and cost metrics are descriptive. Both arms had two drops; fewer calls/tokens do not
establish fewer drops, shorter completion time or sustained post-stop presentation.
Terminal and ever-correct binding are reconstructed from applied actions, independently of success.
Inspect actions clear the active binding; drop does not. Unapplied responses do not establish
binding but count toward inference cost. All four dropped Pig trials had no applied binding.
Initial human readability remains unannotated; Q3(b)'s 210 requests belong to 40 paired trials.
The new paper data export omits credentials, account identifiers, local absolute paths and weights.
Original full evidence remains in the experiment archive; hashes identify exported source records.
