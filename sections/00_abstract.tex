\begin{abstract}
How can a robot find and present a requested object face when it is initially hidden or blocked by the fingers? We present REVEAL, an agentic in-hand manipulation framework connecting visual search, target binding and continuous control. The agent chooses inspection actions, uses new observations to identify the target, and selects a motor goal. A ViserDex-inspired teacher--student system adapted to Revo3 executes these goals, with local controllers adjusting joint commands during reasoning and execution. Paired cube experiments show that fresh images increase success from 9/40 to 32/40 by enabling additional correct bindings. A three-seed controller study supports joint correction during inference. Across six object-specific simulation systems, changed-mapping success averages \QOneChangedSuccess\%, exceeding the strongest evaluated baseline by \QOneChangedGain{} percentage points. English and Chinese instructions are evaluated on three objects.
\end{abstract}
